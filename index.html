About the company

An independent media company that turns information into content people can use.

XD MEDIA LTD is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 12373610. It was incorporated on 20 December 2019 and is registered in Crowthorne, Berkshire.

The company works in information services and digital media. That means writing and publishing content, researching and organising information, and producing material for websites and social channels. The aim is always the same: information that is accurate, well structured and easy to read.

As a small, independent company, XD MEDIA LTD keeps things simple. You deal directly with the people doing the work, and each piece of content is shaped around what you need it to do.

Services

What we do

Three connected areas of work, each focused on making information clearer and more useful.

Content and publishing

Written and visual content for websites, guides and online channels, planned around your audience and written in plain language.

  • Website copy and articles
  • Guides and information resources
  • Editing and restructuring existing content

Information services

Research, collating and organising information into usable formats, so the facts you need are gathered in one clear place.

  • Desk research on a defined topic
  • Collating and summarising sources
  • Organising information into clear documents

Digital media

Content for social and web channels, built for clarity and reach, with a consistent look and voice wherever it appears.

  • Posts and short-form content for social channels
  • Visual content for web pages
  • Consistent formatting across channels

Our approach

How we work

  1. 1

    Agree the brief

    We start with what the content needs to do and who it is for.

  2. 2

    Research and structure

    Information is gathered, checked and arranged into a clear outline before anything is written.

  3. 3

    Create

    Written, visual or social content is produced in a format that suits the channel.

  4. 4

    Review and deliver

    You review the work, we refine it, and you receive files ready to publish.
